About CivicLink

CivicLink is a Human-Computer Interaction based initiative that bridges the communication gap between citizens and municipal authorities. By combining intuitive design, accessibility, and transparency, it empowers communities to take action and promotes smarter, cleaner, and more sustainable cities.
